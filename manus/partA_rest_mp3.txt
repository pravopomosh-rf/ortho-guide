24. [dura_en_DU01] The spinal cord and nerve roots are surrounded by a tough membrane. Inside it is a clear fluid that bathes the nerves.
25. [dura_en_DU02] Sometimes this membrane tears during spine surgery. This is a known complication, especially in repeat operations and when the canal is severely narrowed. Usually the surgeon stitches or seals the tear straight away.
26. [dura_en_DU03] If fluid keeps leaking through the tear, the pressure around the brain drops. This can cause a particular kind of headache. In most cases, once repaired, everything heals without lasting effects.
27. [dura_en_DU04] After a tear, the routine varies. Some patients may get up at the usual time; others are told to lie flat for a while. Once the tear is closed, studies have not shown a convincing benefit of prolonged bed rest over getting up early. But it depends on the situation, so the surgeon decides. Follow your surgeon's instructions.
28. [dura_en_DU05] While lying down, move your feet, do breathing exercises and drink if you are allowed. If clot prevention has been prescribed, do not miss it. Sit or stand up only when allowed, and only with the staff.
29. [dura_en_DU06] Tell the staff if you get a headache that gets worse when sitting or standing and eases when lying down. It may come with nausea, neck pain or ringing in the ears. This can be a sign of a fluid leak.
30. [dura_en_DU07] Tell the staff straight away if your dressing gets wet with clear, watery fluid, if such fluid appears in the drain container, or if a soft swelling appears under the wound. Clear discharge does not always mean a leak, but it must be checked.
31. [dura_en_DU08] Try not to strain. If you are constipated, say so; you may be given a laxative. Do not lift anything heavy until the surgeon allows it.
32. [dura_en_DU09] Tell the medical team immediately if the headache is very severe, or you develop a fever, neck pain and stiffness, sensitivity to light, or confusion. 33. [dura_en_DU10] If these signs appear at home, go to the emergency department immediately. If there is confusion, new marked weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, or another serious condition, call one zero one. Do not drive yourself.
